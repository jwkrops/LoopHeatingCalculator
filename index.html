Aim for
Higher input voltage gives finer regulation on the autotransformer. The loop is deliberately under- or over-compensated to get there.

Fixed: conductor 97.5 °C, XLPE insulation, PE oversheath, start with 10 % extra current, 50 Hz, 1:80 transformers max 230 V, 1:40 max 400 V. Capacitors: 400 V cans, 2 × 497.5 µF each. For background and other options, use the Excel calculator in the office.
